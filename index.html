Frequently asked questions

Who can apply for a Gudu Money loan?

Any salaried or self-employed individual aged 21–58 with a steady source of income can apply, subject to eligibility checks.

Most approved applicants receive funds within 24 hours of final approval, some in as little as 10 minutes.

What documents do I need?

A government-issued ID, address proof, and recent income proof (salary slips or bank statements).

Are there any hidden charges?

No. Your interest rate, processing fee, and total repayment amount are shown upfront before you accept the loan.
