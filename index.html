    Our promise if you fall behind

    Life happens. Here is exactly how we'll behave.

    8am–7pm

    Only in daytime hours

    No calls or messages outside 08:00–19:00 IST. Ever.

    3 a day

    A firm limit on contact

    At most three attempts a day across every channel, two of them calls, and twelve in a week. That is our own policy.

    Just you

    Never your family or employer

    We never contact relatives, employers or references about your loan.

    Paid?

    We check, not argue

    Tell us you've paid and we pause reminders straight away while we find your payment.

    Honest

    Automated means we say so

    If an automated assistant contacts you, it says so in its first sentence and can connect you to a person.

    Ref. no.

    Every complaint is logged

    A complaint gets a reference number the moment you raise it, and a written answer within the deadline.

    3%

    Every loan repaid helps a girl go to school.

    When a loan is repaid, Sonu Marketing sets aside 3% of its processing fee for the education of a girl child. Counted once per loan — quietly, every time.

    Questions

    Straight answers.

    Is Ananya a real person?

    Ananya is our guide on this site. The questions and voice are automated, and we'll always tell you when you are speaking with an automated assistant. You can ask for a person at any time.

    Does checking my estimate affect my credit score?

    No. The estimate on this page runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to us or to a credit bureau.

    What will I actually pay?

    Before you sign, you receive a Key Facts Statement showing the principal, interest, processing fee, any penal charges and the annual percentage rate. Nothing is charged that isn't in it.

    What if I can't pay on time?

    Tell us. If your salary is late, you've lost a job or there's a medical emergency, we record what you tell us, pause reminders where our policy allows, and work out a date with you. See our promise.

    How do I raise a complaint?

    Tell us on any channel. It is logged immediately with a reference number, and you receive a written response within the published deadline. If you're not satisfied, you can escalate to the RBI Ombudsman.
