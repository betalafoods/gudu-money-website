    Questions

    Straight answers.

    Is Ananya a real person?

    Ananya is an automated guide, with recorded Indian English and Hindi narration. For help from a person, use our company contacts.

    Does checking my estimate affect my credit score?

    No. The estimate on this page runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to us or to a credit bureau.

    What will I actually pay?

    Before you sign, you receive a Key Facts Statement showing the principal, interest, processing fee, any penal charges and the annual percentage rate. Nothing is charged that isn't in it.

    What if I can't pay on time?

    Contact the company to discuss your situation and repayment options. Use the published support contacts. This demonstration cannot record requests or change a loan account.

    How do I raise a complaint?

    Contact the published Grievance Officer, Mr. Kathik T, at loans@brahmafinance.in or +91 81236 79365. Ask for the current Grievances Redressal Mechanism and escalation process.
